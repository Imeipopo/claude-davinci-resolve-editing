## Opus 5.5 video prompts (`prompts/third-party/`)

`prompts/third-party/opus55-long-prompts.zh-TW.md` contains Traditional Chinese translations of prompts that their creators shared publicly. Skillry (https://skillry.dev/ai-videos/opus-5-5) publishes them as "Prompts free to copy" and lists them in https://github.com/yihui-dev/awesome-opus5-5-videos. **Each prompt belongs to its creator and is not covered by this repository's MIT License.** Every entry links the creator's original post. Creators who want an entry changed or removed can open an issue and we will do so. The translations and "寫法拆解" commentary were written for this repository.
